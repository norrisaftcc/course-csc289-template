# ALGOCRATIC GROWTH-FRAME POSTER SET
## Environmental Anchoring Materials

**Purpose:** Reinforce growth frame through environmental cues  
**Placement:** Front of classroom, near door, eye level  
**Rotation:** Swap sets monthly to prevent habituation  
**Design Note:** These are text-ready. Pair with AlgoCratic branding, dark/muted color scheme, corporate dystopian aesthetic.

## DESIGN NOTES

### Visual Style
- Dark background (black, dark grey, dark blue)
- Light text (white, light grey)
- Minimal color accent (orange, red, or cyan for emphasis)
- Corporate/institutional feel, not playful
- Clean sans-serif fonts
- Generous whitespace

### Size
- Primary posters: 18x24" or 24x36"
- Daily reminders (Series 5): 11x17" or 8.5x11"

### Production
- Print shop quality preferred
- Laminate for durability
- Frame optional but adds legitimacy

### DIY Option
- Black foam board
- White vinyl lettering
- Or: white paper, dark border, simple typography

---

## THE META POINT

These aren't decorations. They're environmental anchors.

Every time a student reads "The Algorithm measures Δ," they're being reminded—at a level below conscious argument—that improvement is what matters.

The repetition is the point. The AlgoCratic frame is the delivery mechanism.

The message underneath all of these:

**You can improve. We're watching for it. It counts.**
